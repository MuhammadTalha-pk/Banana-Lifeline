## Where bananas are grown

The banana needs heat, humid air, plenty of water, light. It dislikes wind. It likes soils rich in organic matter. It likes soils that drain quickly.
Once picked, bananas should not be left long in the plantation. Banana plants are planted along roads, tracks, railway lines or lagoons so that the fruits can be quickly moved away.
In the world as a whole, Central and South America produce most bananas - nearly 20 million tons a year. Asia produces 10.2 million tons and Africa 4.2 million tons.
